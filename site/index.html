As my skillset expanded, I moved into managing regional technical staff. I focused on clarity, calm decision‑making, and building trust across distributed teams. Leading engineers became as natural to me as supporting systems.

Alongside leadership, I’ve invested heavily in modern cloud‑native engineering — automation, observability, Kubernetes, and platform design. I’ve been practicing hands‑on by building out my portfolio, sharpening the skills that define my next chapter.
